# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want tiny-browse to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Trolling or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at prakashshubham36@gmail.com. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code.
